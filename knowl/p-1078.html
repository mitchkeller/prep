Recall that the volume of a cone of radius \(r\) and height \(h\) is \(V = \frac{\pi}{3} r^2 h\text{.}\) Due to the shape of the tank, similar triangles tell us that \(r\) and \(h\) satisfy the proportion \(r = \frac{1}{2}h\text{,}\) and thus

\begin{equation} V = \frac{\pi}{3} \left( \frac{1}{2}h \right)^2 h = \frac{\pi}{12}h^3 \text{.}\tag{1.7.1} \end{equation}
