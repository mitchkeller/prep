Solution 2.2.5.2.1.

SOLUTION To convert \(45^{\circ}\) to radians, multiply by \(\pi/180^{\circ}\text{:}\) \(\displaystyle 45^{\circ} \cdot \left( \frac{\pi}{180^{\circ}} \right) = \frac{45 \pi}{180}\) Thus we say that the radian measure of a \(45^{\circ}\) angle is \(\frac{45 \pi}{180}\) which is approximately 0.785398 radians.
