Exercise 5.

Suppose that we want a cylindrical barrel to hold \(8\) cubic feet of volume. Let the barrel have radius \(r\) and height \(h\text{,}\) each measured in feet. How can we write the surface area, \(A\text{,}\) of the barrel solely as a function of \(r\text{?}\)

  1. Draw several possible pictures of how the barrel might look. For instance, what if the radius is very small? How will the height appear in comparison? Likewise, what happens if the height is very small?

  2. Use the fact that volume is fixed at \(8\) cubic feet to state a constraint equation and solve that equation for \(h\) in terms of \(r\text{.}\)

  3. Recall that the surface area of a cylinder is \(A = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh\text{.}\) Use your work in (c) to write \(A\) as a function of only \(r\text{.}\)

  4. What is the domain of \(A\text{?}\) Why?

  5. Explain why \(A\) is not a polynomial function of \(r\text{.}\)
