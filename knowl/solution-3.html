SOLUTION (a) At 80 mph, fuel consumption is about 24 mpg, so fuel used is \(400 / 23.9188079597599 \approx 16.7 \ \mbox{gallons}\text{.}\) (b) At 60 mph, fuel consumption is about 30 mpg, so fuel used on a 600 mile trip is \(600 / 30 \approx 20.2 \mbox{ gallons}\text{.}\) At 70 mph, fuel consumption is about 27 mpg, so fuel used on this trip is \(600 / 27 \approx 22.1 \mbox{ gallons}\text{.}\) Altogether we would then save \(22.1 - 20.2 \approx 1.9 \mbox{ gallons}\text{.}\) (c) The most fuel efficient speed is where mpg is a maximum, which is about 55 mph.
