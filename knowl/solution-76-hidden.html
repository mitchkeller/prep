SOLUTION\(G(11)\) is the output corresponding to the input of \(t=11\text{.}\) So \(G(11)\) represents the GDP for the year 2001 (11 years after 1990). The input to the \(G^{-1}\) function is billions of dollars, so its output is a time in years after 1990. Thus, \(G^{-1}(9873)\) represents the number of years after 1990 at which the GDP is 9873 billion dollars.
