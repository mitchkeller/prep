Point your browser to the Desmos worksheet at http://gvsu.edu/s/0zy 1 . There you'll find a degree \(4\) polynomial of the form \(p(x) = a_0 + a_1x + a_2x^2 + a_3x^3 + a_4x^4\text{,}\) where \(a_0, \ldots, a_4\) are set up as sliders. In the questions that follow, you'll experiment with different values of \(a_0, \ldots, a_4\) to investigate different possible behaviors in a degree \(4\) polynomial.
