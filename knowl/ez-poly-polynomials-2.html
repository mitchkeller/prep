Exercise 9.

Consider the (non-polynomial) function \(r(x) = e^{-x^2}(x^2+1)(x-2)(x-3)\text{.}\)

  1. What are the zeros of \(r(x)\text{?}\) (Hint: is \(e^{\Box}\) ever equal to zero?)

  2. Construct a sign chart for \(r(x)\text{.}\)

  3. Plot \(r(x)\) in Desmos. Is the sign and overall behavior of \(r\) obvious from the plot? Why or why not?

  4. From the graph, what appears to be the value of \(\lim_{x \to \infty} r(x)\text{?}\) Why is this surprising in light of the behavior of \(f(x)=(x^2+1)(x-2)(x-3)\) as \(x \to \infty\text{?}\)
