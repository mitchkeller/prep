SOLUTION (a) Since \(N\) is growing by 9% per year, we know that \(N\) is an exponential function of \(t\) with growth factor \(1 + 0.09 = 1.09\text{.}\) Since \(N = 7.2\) when \(t = 0\text{,}\) we have
\begin{equation*} N = 7.2(1.09)^t. \end{equation*}
(b) In the year 2011, we have \(t = 8\) and
\begin{equation*} N = 7.2(1.09)^{8} \approx 14.347 \hbox{ million passengers.} \end{equation*}
(c) In the year 2000, we have \(t = -3\) and
\begin{equation*} N = 7.2(1.09)^{-3} \approx 5.56 \hbox{ million passengers.} \end{equation*}
