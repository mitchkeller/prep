It is often useful to follow one particular point through a sequence of transformations. In Figure 2.4.1, we see the red point that is located at \((0,1)\) on the original function \(y = \cos(t)\text{,}\) as well as the point \((b, a+c)\) that is the corresponding point on \(k(t) = a\cos(t-b) + c\) under the overall transformation. Note that the point \((b,a+c)\) results from the input, \(t = b\text{,}\) that makes the argument of the cosine function zero: \(k(b) = a\cos(b-b) + c = a\cos(0) + c\text{.}\)
