Solution 1.6.5.3.1.

SOLUTION (a) To write \(s\) as a function of \(A\text{,}\) we solve \(A=6s^2\) for \(s\)
\begin{equation*} s^2=\frac{A}{6}\qquad\hbox{ so} \qquad s=f(A)=+\sqrt{\frac{A}{6}} \end{equation*}
Because the length of a side of a cube is positive. The function \(f\) gives the side of a cube in terms of its area \(A\text{.}\) (b) Substituting \(s=f(A)=\sqrt{A/6}\) in the formula \(V=g(s)=s^3\) gives the volume, \(V\text{,}\) as a function of surface area, \(A\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} V=g(f(A))=s^3=\left(\sqrt{\frac{A}{6}}\right)^3. \end{equation*}
