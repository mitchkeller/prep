SOLUTION The formula \(f(t) = ab^t\) represents exponential growth if the base \(b > 1\) and exponential decay if \(0 \lt b \lt 1\text{.}\) Towns 2, 5, 4 are growing and towns 6, 1, 3 are shrinking. Town 4 is growing the fastest since its growth factor of 1.187 is the largest. Since 1.187 - 1 = 0.187, it is growing at a rate of 18.7%. Town 1 is shrinking the fastest since its decay factor of 0.78 is the smallest. Since 1 - 0.78 = 0.22, it is shrinking at a rate of 22%. In the exponential function \(f(t) = ab^t\text{,}\) the parameter \(a\) gives the value of the function when \(t = 0\text{.}\) We see that town 6 has the largest initial population (2800) and town 5 has the smallest initial population (500).
