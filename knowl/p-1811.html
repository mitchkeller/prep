On the other hand, if we let \(h(t) = f(\frac{1}{2}t) = \sin(\frac{1}{2}t)\text{,}\) the transformed graph \(h\) is stretched away from the \(y\)-axis by a factor of \(2\text{.}\) This has the effect of doubling the period of \(f\text{,}\) so that the period of \(h\) is \(P = 2 \cdot 2\pi = 4\pi\text{,}\) as seen in Figure 2.4.6.
