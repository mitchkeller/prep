We are given the fact that the tank is being filled in such a way that the height of the water rises at a constant rate of \(0.4\) feet per minute. Said differently, \(h\) is a function of \(t\) whose average rate of change is constant. What kind of function does this make \(h = p(t)\text{?}\) Determine a formula for \(p(t)\text{.}\)
