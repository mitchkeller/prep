If we pick any point \((x,y)\) that lies on the unit circle, the point is associated with a right triangle whose horizontal leg has length \(|x|\) and whose vertical leg has length \(|y|\text{,}\) as seen in Figure 2.2.1. By the Pythagorean Theorem, it follows that

\begin{equation*} x^2 + y^2 = 1\text{,} \end{equation*}

and this is the equation of the unit circle: a point \((x,y)\) lies on the unit circle if and only if \(x^2 + y^2 = 1\text{.}\)
