Exercise 3.

Let \(\displaystyle r(x) = \frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\text{,}\) where \(p\) and \(q\) are polynomials of degrees \(m\) and \(n\) respectively.

(a) If \(r(x) \to 0\) as \(x \to \infty\text{,}\) then

  • \(\displaystyle m > n\)

  • \(\displaystyle m = n\)

  • \(\displaystyle m \lt n\)

  • None of the above

(b) If \(r(x) \to k\) as \(x \to \infty\text{,}\) with \(k \not= 0\text{,}\) then

  • \(\displaystyle m \lt n\)

  • \(\displaystyle m > n\)

  • \(\displaystyle m = n\)

  • None of the above
