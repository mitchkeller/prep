In Section 2.3, we defined the cosine and sine functions as the functions that track the location of a point traversing the unit circle counterclockwise from \((1,0)\text{.}\) In particular, for a central angle of radian measure \(t\) that passes through the point \((1,0)\text{,}\) we define \(\cos(t)\) as the \(x\)-coordinate of the point where the other side of the angle intersects the unit circle, and \(\sin(t)\) as the \(y\)-coordinate of that same point, as pictured in Figure 4.1.1.
