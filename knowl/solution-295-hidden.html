SOLUTION Start with our property of inverse functions \(f \left( f^{-1}(x) \right) = x\text{,}\) and substitute \(y\) for \(f^{-1}(x)\) to get \(f(y)=x\text{.}\) Now, using the formula for \(f\) we get \(\displaystyle f(y) = \frac{6 y - 6}{7 y + 4} =x\) and solving for \(y\) yields
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{6 y - 6}{7 y + 4} \amp =x \\ 6 y -6 \amp = x(7 y+4) \\ 6 y -6 \amp = 7 xy + 4 x \\ 6 y- 7 yx \amp = 4 x +6 \\ y(6- 7 x) \amp = 4 x +6 \\ y \amp = \frac{4 x + 6}{6 - 7 x}. \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
Now replacing \(y\) by \(f^{-1}(x)\text{,}\) we have our formula,
\begin{equation*} f^{-1}(x) = \frac{4 x + 6}{6 - 7 x}. \end{equation*}
