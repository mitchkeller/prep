A related situation arises when an object's temperature changes in response to its surroundings. For instance, if we have a cup of coffee at an initial temperature of \(186^\circ\) Fahrenheit and the cup is placed in a room where the surrounding temperature is \(71^\circ\text{,}\) our intuition and experience tell us that over time the coffee will cool and eventually tend to the \(71^\circ\) temperature of the surroundings.
