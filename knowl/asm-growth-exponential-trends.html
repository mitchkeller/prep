Trends in exponential function behavior.

For an exponential function of the form \(f(t) = ab^t\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are both positive with \(b \ne 1\text{,}\)

  • if \(b \gt 1\text{,}\) then \(f\) is always increasing and always increases at an increasing rate;

  • if \(0 \lt b \lt 1\text{,}\) then \(f\) is always decreasing and always decreases at an increasing rate.
