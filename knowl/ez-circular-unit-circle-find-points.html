Exercise 8.

Let \((x,y)\) by a point on the unit circle. In each of the following situations, determine requested value exactly.

  1. Suppose that \(x = -0.3\) and \(y\) is negative. Find the value of \(y\text{.}\)

  2. Suppose that \((x,y)\) lies in Quadrant II and \(x = -2y\text{.}\) Find the values of \(x\) and \(y\text{.}\)

  3. Suppose that \((x,y)\) lies a distance of \(\frac{29\pi}{6}\) units clockwise around the circle from \((1,0)\text{.}\) Find the values of \(x\) and \(y\text{.}\)

  4. At what exact point(s) does the line \(y = \frac{1}{2}x + \frac{1}{2}\) intersect the unit circle?
