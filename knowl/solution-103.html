Solution 2.1.5.2.1.

SOLUTION (a) Since the ferris wheel makes one full rotation every 14 minutes, the period is 14 minutes. (b) The midline is the height when you are halfway up the ferris wheel. If the ferris wheel has a diameter of 140 meters and the loading platform is 8 meters above the ground, the height midway up the ferris wheel is \(\frac{140}{2} + 8 = 78\text{.}\) So the midline is \(h = 78\) meters. (c) The amplitude is the difference of your height on top of the ferris wheel and your height at the midline (or halfway up the ferris wheel). When you are highest on the ferris wheel, your height is \(140 + 8 = 148\text{.}\) Therefore the amplitude is \(148 - 78 = 70\) meters. (d) Based on the answers above, the correct answer is graph B.
