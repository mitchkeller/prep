From the graph, we see that \(g(t) = \sin(2t)\) oscillates twice as frequently as \(f(t) = \sin(t)\text{,}\) and that \(g\) completes a full cycle on the interval \([0,\pi]\text{,}\) which is half the length of the period of \(f\text{.}\) Thus, the “\(2\)” in \(f(2t)\) causes the period of \(f\) to be \(\frac{1}{2}\) as long; specifially, the period of \(g\) is \(P = \frac{1}{2} (2\pi) = \pi\text{.}\)
