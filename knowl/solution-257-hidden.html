SOLUTION

(a) To find the roots, we solve \(x^{2}\!\left(7-8x^{9}\right) = 0\) for \(x\text{.}\) Either \(x^{2} = 0\text{,}\) in which case \(x = 0\text{,}\) or \(7 - 8 x^{9} = 0\text{,}\) in which case \(\displaystyle x = \sqrt[9]{\frac{7}{8}}\text{.}\)

(b) Since the leading term is \(-8 x^{11}\) and \(-8 x^{11} \to -\infty\) as \(x \to \infty\text{,}\) we have \(x^{2}\!\left(7-8x^{9}\right) \to -\infty\) as \(x \to \infty\text{.}\)

(c) Since the leading term is \(-8 x^{11}\) and \(-8 x^{11} \to \infty\) as \(x \to -\infty\text{,}\) we have \(x^{2}\!\left(7-8x^{9}\right) \to \infty\) as \(x \to -\infty\text{.}\)
