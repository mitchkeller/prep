The \(y\)-coordinate of the point on the graph of \(g(x)\) (the dashed line) when \(x = 6.1\) is \(y = 4.9\text{.}\) Similarly, the \(x\)-coordinate of the point on the graph of \(g(x)\) (the dashed line) when \(y = 2.9\) is \(x = 2.2\text{.}\) (c) There are three points on the graph of \(f(x)\) which have a \(y\)-coordinate of 2.9: \(x = 2.2, 5.2\text{,}\) and \(6.9\text{.}\) (d) Two functions are equal when their graphs intersect. The graphs of \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) intersect at two points, when \(x = 2.2\) and \(6.1\text{.}\)
