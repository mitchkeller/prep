  3. What is the exact value of \(\sin(\theta)\text{?}\)

  4. Rewrite your equation from (b) using the arcsine function in the form \(\arcsin(\Box) = \Delta\text{,}\) where \(\Box\) and \(\Delta\) are numerical values.
