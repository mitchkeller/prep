Recall our work in Section 1.8, where we studied how the graph of the function \(g\) defined by \(g(x) = af(x-b) + c\) is related to the graph of \(f\text{,}\) where \(a\text{,}\) \(b\text{,}\) and \(c\) are real numbers with \(a \ne 0\text{.}\) Because such transformations can shift and stretch a function, we are interested in understanding how we can use transformations of the sine and cosine functions to fit formulas to circular functions.
