SOLUTION

(a) To find the roots, we solve \(\left(5-6x\right)\!\left(2x-4\right)^{2} = 0\) for \(x\text{.}\) Either \(5 - 6 x = 0\text{,}\) in which case \(\displaystyle x = \frac{5}{6}\text{,}\) or \((2 x - 4)^2 = 0\text{,}\) in which case \(\displaystyle x = \frac{4}{2}}\text{.}\)

(b) Since the leading term is \(-24 x^3\) and \(-24 x^3 \to -\infty\) as \(x \to \infty\text{,}\) we have \(\left(5-6x\right)\!\left(2x-4\right)^{2} \to -\infty\) as \(x \to \infty\text{.}\)

(c) Since the leading term is \(-24 x^3\) and \(-24 x^3 \to \infty\) as \(x \to -\infty\text{,}\) we have \(\left(5-6x\right)\!\left(2x-4\right)^{2} \to \infty\) as \(x \to -\infty\text{.}\)
