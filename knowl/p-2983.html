  1. When the balloon is \(200\) feet off the ground, what is the angle of elevation of the camera?

  2. When the balloon is \(275\) feet off the ground, what is the angle of elevation of the camera?

  3. Let \(\theta\) represent the camera's angle of elevation when the balloon is at an arbitrary height \(h\) above the ground. Express \(\theta\) as a function of \(h\text{.}\)

  4. Determine \(AV_{[200,275]}\) for \(\theta\) (as a function of \(h\)) and write at least one sentence to carefully explain the meaning of the value you find, including units.
