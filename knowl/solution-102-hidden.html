SOLUTION (a) The graph appears to have a period of 60. Every change in the \(x\) value of 60 brings us back to the same \(y\) value. (b) The midline of the graph is 7 since
\begin{equation*} \mbox{midline} = \frac{ y_{max} + y_{min} }{2} = \frac{ 17 - 3 }{2} = 7. \end{equation*}
(c) The amplitude is how much the maximum or minimum \(y\) value differs from the average (midline). Since the maximum \(y\) value is 17 and the midline is 7, the amplitude is \(17 - 7 = 10\text{.}\)
