Solution 1.7.5.4.1.

SOLUTION (a) The perimeter of a square of side length \(s\) is \(P = f(s) = 4s\text{.}\) (b) Thus, \(f(4) = 16\text{,}\) which is the perimeter of a square of side length 4. (c) The inverse function \(s = f^{-1}(P) = P/4\text{,}\) so \(f^{-1}(32) = 8\text{,}\) which is the side length of a square of perimeter 32. (d) The inverse function is obtained by solving \(P = f(s) = 4s\) for \(s = f^{-1}(P) = P/4\text{.}\)
