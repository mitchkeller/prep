Solution 5.4.5.3.1.

SOLUTION (a) If \(\lim_{x\to\infty}r(x)=0\text{,}\) then the degree of the denominator is greater than the degree of the numerator, so \(n>m\text{.}\) (b) If \(\lim_{x\to\infty} r(x)=k\text{,}\) with \(k\ne0\text{,}\) the degree of the numerator and denominator must be equal, so \(n=m\text{.}\)
