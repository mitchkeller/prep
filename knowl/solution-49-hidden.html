SOLUTION

We can solve the equation \(4\!\left(x+8\right)^{2}-8 = 0\) algebraically.

\begin{equation*} \begin{array}{rcl} 4\!\left(x+8\right)^{2}-8 \amp = \amp 0 \\ 4(x+8)^2 \amp = \amp + 8 \\ (x+8)^2 \amp = \amp + 2 \\ x+8 \amp = \amp \pm \sqrt{ + 2} \\ x \amp = \amp -8 \pm \sqrt{ + 2}. \end{array} \end{equation*}
