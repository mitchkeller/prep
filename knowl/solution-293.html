Solution 5.5.4.2.1.

SOLUTION Since the graph has an asymptote at \(x=4\text{,}\) let the denominator be \((x-4)\text{.}\) Since the graph has a zero at \(x= 1\text{,}\) let the numerator be \((x-1)\text{.}\) Since the long-run behavior tends toward \(y = -1\) as \(x \to \pm \infty\text{,}\) the ratio of the leading terms should be \(-1\text{.}\) So a possible formula is \(\displaystyle y = f(x) = - \frac{x-1}{x-4}\text{.}\) You can check that the \(y\)-intercept is \(y = -0.25\text{,}\) as it should be.
