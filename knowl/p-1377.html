Certain naturally occurring phenomena eventually repeat themselves, especially when the phenomenon is somehow connected to a circle. For example, suppose that you are taking a ride on a ferris wheel and we consider your height, \(h\text{,}\) above the ground and how your height changes in tandem with the distance, \(d\text{,}\) that you have traveled around the wheel. In Figure 2.1.1 we see a snapshot of this situation, which is available as a full animation 1  at http://gvsu.edu/s/0Dt 2 .
