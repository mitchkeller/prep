SOLUTION Since the function is linear, we can choose any two points (from the graph) to find its formula. We use the form \(p = b + mh\) to get the price of an apartment as a function of its height. We use the two points (10, 190000) and (20, 230000). We begin by finding the slope
\begin{equation*} m = \frac{ \Delta p}{\Delta h} = \frac{230000 - 190000}{20 - 10} = 4000 \end{equation*}
Next, we substitute a point into our equation using our slope of 4000 dollars per meter of height and solve for \(b\text{,}\) the \(p\)-intercept. We use the point ( 10, 190000):
\begin{equation*} 190000 = b + 4000 \cdot 10 \end{equation*}
so \(150000 = b\text{.}\) Therefore, \(p = f(h) = 150000 + 4000 h\text{.}\)
