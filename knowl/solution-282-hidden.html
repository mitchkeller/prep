SOLUTION There is a horizontal asymptote at \(y = 5\text{,}\) so \(\displaystyle \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = 5\) and \(\displaystyle \lim_{x \to - \infty} f(x) = 5\text{.}\) There is a vertical asymptote at \(x = 1\) such that \(\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 1^+} f(x) = \infty\) and \(\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 1^-} f(x) = \infty\text{.}\)
