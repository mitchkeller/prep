SOLUTION The arc length, \(s\text{,}\) corresponding to an angle of \(\theta\) radians in a circle of radius \(r\) is \(s = r \cdot \theta\text{.}\) In order to change from degrees to radians, we multiply the number of degrees by \(\frac{\pi}{180}\text{,}\) so we have \(90^{\circ} \cdot \frac{\pi}{180}\) radians. Thus our arc length is \(s = 9.42478\)
