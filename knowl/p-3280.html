Observe that in Figure 5.2.9, the up-close plot near the zero \(x = 0\) of multiplicity \(3\text{,}\) the polynomial function \(g\) looks similar to the basic cubic polynomial \(-x^3\text{.}\) In addition, in Figure 5.2.10, we observe that if we zoom in even futher on the zero of multiplicity \(1\text{,}\) the function \(g\) looks roughly linear, like a degree \(1\) polynomial. This type of behavior near repeated zeros turns out to hold in other cases as well.
