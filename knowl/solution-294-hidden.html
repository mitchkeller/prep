SOLUTION Since the graph has a vertical asymptotes at \(x=4\) and \(x = -3\text{,}\) let the denominator be \((x-4)(x + 3)\text{.}\) Since the graph has zeros at \(x= 5\) and \(x = -4\) let the numerator be \((x-5)(x + 4)\text{.}\) Since the long-run behavior tends toward \(y = -1\) as \(x \to \pm \infty\text{,}\) the ratio of the leading terms should be \(-1\text{.}\) So a possible formula is \(\displaystyle y = f(x) = -\frac{(x-5)(x + 4)}{(x-4)(x + 3)}\text{.}\) You can check that the \(y\)-intercept is \(y = -\frac{5}{3}\text{,}\) as it should be.
