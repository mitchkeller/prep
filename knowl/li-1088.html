Item.

Anytime we know two side lengths in a right triangle, we can use one of the inverse trigonometric functions to determine the measure of one of the non-right angles. For instance, if we know the values of \(\text{opp}\) and \(\text{adj}\) in Figure 4.4.3, then since

\begin{equation*} \tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\text{,} \end{equation*}

it follows that \(\theta = \arctan(\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}})\text{.}\)

If we instead know the hypotenuse and one of the two legs, we can use either the arcsine or arccosine function accordingly.
