Suppose that \(a\text{,}\) \(b\) and \(c\) are the sides of a right triangle, where side \(a\) is across from angle \(A\text{,}\) side \(b\) is across from angle \(B\text{,}\) and side \(c\) is across from the right angle. If \(a = 17\) and \(B = 33^{\circ}\text{,}\) find the missing sides and angles in this right triangle. All angles should be in degrees (not radians), and all trig functions entered will be evaluated in degrees (not radians).
