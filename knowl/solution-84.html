Solution 1.8.5.5.1.

SOLUTION (a) The translation \(f(x-6)\) moves the graph to the right 6 units. The \(y\)-coordinate is not changed, but the \(x\)-coordinate is \(9 + 6 = 15\text{,}\) and the new point is therefore (15, 4). (b) The translation \(f(x)-5\) moves the graph down 5 units. The \(x\)-coordinate is not changed, but the \(y\)-coordinate is \(4 - 5 = -1\text{,}\) and the new point is therefore (9, -1). (c) The translation \(f(x+2) + 7\) moves the graph both to the left 2 units and up 7 units. Both coordinates will therefore change. The \(x\)-coordinate is \(9 - 2 = 7\text{,}\) the \(y\)-coordinate is \(4 + 7 = 11\text{,}\) and the new point is therefore (7, 11).
