SOLUTION (a) We assume that the price of a movie ticket increases at the rate of 3.8% per year. This means that the price is rising exponentially, so a formula for \(p\) is of the form \(p=ab^t\text{.}\) We have \(a=9.00\) and \(b=1+r=1.038\text{.}\) Thus, a formula for \(p\) is
\begin{equation*} p = 9.00(1.038)^t. \end{equation*}
(b) In \(30\) years \(t=30\) we have \(p = 9.00(1.038)^{30} \approx 27.55\text{.}\)
