Solution 2.3.6.4.1.

SOLUTION a) The coordinate of the point \(S\) are \((x_0,y_0) = ( a, \sin{(a)} )\) while the coordinates of the point \(T\) are \((x_1,y_1) = ( a+h, \sin{(a+h)} )\text{.}\) Thus the slope between these two points can be expressed as \(\displaystyle \mbox{slope} = \frac{ y_1 - y_0 }{x_1-x_0} = \frac{ \sin{(a+h)} - \sin{(a)} }{ (a+h) - a} = \frac{ \sin{(a+h)} - \sin{(a)} }{h}\) b) Plugging in the values \(a = 1.6\) and \(h = 0.01\) into the expression above you find \(\mbox{slope} \approx -0.03\text{.}\)
