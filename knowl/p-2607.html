By changing our perspective slightly, we can see that it is equivalent to think of the values of the sine and cosine function as representing the lengths of legs in right triangles. Specifically, given a central angle 1  \(\theta\text{,}\) if we think of the right triangle with vertices \((\cos(\theta),0)\text{,}\) \((0,0)\text{,}\) and \((\cos(\theta), \sin(\theta))\text{,}\) then the length of the horizontal leg is \(\cos(\theta)\) and the length of the vertical leg is \(\sin(\theta)\text{,}\) as seen in Figure 4.1.2.
