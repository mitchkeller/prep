SOLUTION (a) \(r(5) = 600 - 30(5)=450\text{.}\) (b) \(r(5) = 450\) means water is entering the reservoir at 450 gallons per second at time \(t = 5\) seconds. (c) The vertical intercept first tells us that at time \(t = 0\) seconds, the water is initially flowing in at the rate of 600 gallons per second. The horizontal intercept tells us that at \(600/30 \approx 20\) seconds, the flow has stopped. The slope is \(-30\text{.}\) This means that the rate at which water enters the reservoir decreases by 30 gallons per second each second. The water is flowing in at a decreasing rate. (d) The reservoir has more and more water when the rate, \(r(t)\text{,}\) is positive because water is being added. Water is being added until \(t = 600/30\) seconds later when it starts flowing out. This means at \(t = 600/30\text{,}\) the most water is in the reservoir. (e) The reservoir has water draining out between \(t= 600/30\) and \(t= 30\text{,}\) but this amount is not as much as the water that entered between \(t=0\) and \(t=600/30\text{.}\) Thus, the reservoir had the least amount of water at the beginning when \(t=0\text{.}\) Remember the function \(r(t)\) gives the rate of flow, not the amount of water in the reservoir. (f) If the domain is specified as \(0 \leq t \leq 30\) , then the rate varies from 600 gallons per second at \(t=0\) to -300 gallons per second at \(t=30\text{,}\) so the range is \(-300 \leq r(t) \leq 600\text{.}\) In interval notation we express the range as \(\lbrack -300, 600 \rbrack\) .
