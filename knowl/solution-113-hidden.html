SOLUTION To convert \(22^{\circ}\) to radians, multiply by \(\pi/180^{\circ}\text{:}\) \(\displaystyle 22^{\circ} \cdot \left( \frac{\pi}{180^{\circ}} \right) = \frac{22 \pi}{180}\) Thus we say that the radian measure of a \(22^{\circ}\) angle is \(\frac{22 \pi}{180}\) which is approximately 0.383972 radians.
