Exercise 6.

Consider a right triangle where the two legs measure \(5\) and \(12\) respectively and \(\alpha\) is the angle opposite the shorter leg and \(\beta\) is the angle opposite the longer leg.

  1. What is the exact value of \(\cos(\alpha)\text{?}\)

  2. What is the exact value of \(\sin(\beta)\text{?}\)

  3. What is the exact value of \(\tan(\beta)\text{?}\) of \(\tan(\alpha)\text{?}\)

  4. What is the exact radian measure of \(\alpha\text{?}\) approximate measure?

  5. What is the exact radian measure of \(\beta\text{?}\) approximate measure?

  6. True or false: for any two angles \(\theta\) and \(\gamma\) such that \(\theta + \gamma = \frac{\pi}{2}\) (radians), it follows that \(\cos(\theta) = \sin(\gamma)\text{.}\)
