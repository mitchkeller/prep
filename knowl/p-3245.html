We know that each of the power functions \(x\text{,}\) \(x^2\text{,}\) \(\ldots\text{,}\) \(x^n\) grow without bound as \(x \to \infty\text{.}\) Intuitively, we sense that \(x^5\) grows faster than \(x^4\) (and likewise for any comparison of a higher power to a lower one). This means that for large values of \(x\text{,}\) the most important term in any polynomial is its highest order term, as we saw in Preview Activity 5.2.1 when we compared \(p(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + a_3 x^3 + a_4 x^4\) and \(y = a_4 x^4\text{.}\)
