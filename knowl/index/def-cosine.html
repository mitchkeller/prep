Definition 2.3.6.

Given a central angle in the unit circle that measures \(t\) radians and that intersects the circle at both \((1,0)\) and \((a,b)\text{,}\) as shown in Figure 2.3.7, we define the cosine of \(t\), denoted \(\cos(t)\text{,}\) by the rule
\begin{equation*} \cos(t) = a\text{.} \end{equation*}
