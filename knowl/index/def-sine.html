Definition 2.3.2.

Given a central angle in the unit circle that measures \(t\) radians and that intersects the circle at both \((1,0)\) and \((a,b)\text{,}\) as shown in Figure 2.3.3, we define the sine of \(t\), denoted \(\sin(t)\text{,}\) by the rule
\begin{equation*} \sin(t) = b\text{.} \end{equation*}
